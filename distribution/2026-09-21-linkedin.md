## Body (Rodney's final, LinkedIn editor, 21 September 2026, 21:37)

Updated this week: the Agent 365 field guide for security and compliance engineers.

Three things moved on Microsoft Learn, and the guide moved with them:

• **Defender for Endpoint** now discovers **local AI agents and their MCP servers** on onboarded Windows and macOS devices, with no additional deployment. Still in public preview. agent-365-guide/#s5-5

• **Intune** now has a **native policy** that **enables Defender's prompt-injection protection** for supported **local AI agents without a custom script**. A **PowerShell** script is still required when Defender has to inspect an agent's network traffic instead. agent-365-guide/#s7-4

• **Snowflake Cortex agents** can be synced into the **Agent 365 registry**. agent-365-guide/#s5-4

• Also new to the guide: **a table that starts from your scenario** ("a developer installs Claude Code on a laptop") and lands on the control, the product that enforces it, and its status today. agent-365-guide/#s4-1

Every claim in this guide links to Learn. GA/Preview status badges follow Microsoft's release notes, checked every Monday.

See what changed and what you can deploy today: https://rodneymhungu.github.io/agent-365-guide/#s10
