Which surface actually sees Claude Code, Cursor or OpenClaw on a laptop, and which one can block it?

Four Microsoft surfaces list local AI agents, and they do not agree: the Shadow AI page, the Local agents page, Defender for Endpoint discovery and runtime protection. I joined their four lists into one table, every cell from the Learn page it comes from.

Two things fall out. Blocking from the admin centre reaches only the Node.js family and the VS Code extensions. And Defender for Endpoint sees the widest set, so the inventory starts there.
